## Do

**Part 1 — search keywords per key section (then STOP and wait).**

1. Choose the **key sections** for proof (about 5): the hero, the section that carries the USP, one proof section, one content-heavy section, and the one closest to the CTA. Show the list with `S##`.
2. For each, give **search keywords** the user can paste into a reference gallery (e.g. a design-inspiration site), tied to the pattern in Step 08 — plus 1–2 references to avoid (do-not-copy list).
3. Tell the user: collect 3–6 screenshots per key section into `02-assets/moodboard/screens/S##-name/`, then say **"references done"**. **STOP and wait.**

**Part 2 — read the references (after "references done").**

4. Read every image. For each key section write: the layout pattern, the visual hierarchy, spacing density, the type and imagery treatment, and **what we take and what we do not**. State how the pattern will use our palette and font, never theirs.
5. Check each section against the caps from Step 12 (boxes, CTAs) and the focal point: if a reference shows many boxes, say how ours will use fewer.
6. **Icons and fonts** — confirm the icon library and fonts (from Step 10, or the assets), with licences stated.
