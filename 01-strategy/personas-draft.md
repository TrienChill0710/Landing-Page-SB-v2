## 1. Target audience statement

The ShopBase sheet says there are several segments (newbie, oldier, guru) but ShopBase's target audience is the group that:
- already has experience and knowledge of the industry,
- controls its own supplier (marked optional, because the sheet says ShopBase also offers fulfillment for dropshipping (PlusHub) and POD (PrintHub), but at a price the sheet says is less competitive than Chinese suppliers; `SUPERSEDED/UNKNOWN`: PlusHub and PrintHub are named after the retired PlusBase and PrintBase, and the sources do not say whether this fulfillment still exists in ShopBase 2.0, so it does not drive Home),
- controls its own payment gateway (receives payments from international customers). [SB-R2 Dropshipping] [SB-R3 POD]

Newbie groups are outside the primary target because the ShopBase sheet's target audience is "already has experience and knowledge of the industry" [SB-R2 Dropshipping]. (Note: "future use" in the lead table is not a reason. Every lead-table row, including the experienced groups, carries "(future use)" after its lead status [LEAD-1]–[LEAD-6].)

Draft statement (updated per user decision, 2026-10-24): *Experienced dropshipping and print-on-demand sellers, based in Vietnam, who sell to buyers in the US and EU, run their own payment setup and often source from their own suppliers.* "Sell to international buyers" comes from "receives payments from international customers" [SB-R2 Dropshipping] and from US/EU buyers in [SB-R5 VN Seller]. "Vietnam-based" comes from the user's decision and the "VN Seller" row [SB-R5 VN Seller]. This settles the earlier Vietnam-vs-global conflict: the audience is Vietnam-based sellers selling abroad; Home copy stays English. Open point: how Vietnam-specific items (for example Vietnamese-language support) appear on an English page (Q3).

## 2. Primary personas

The sheet has one shared empathy map (rows 11-16), not one per type. The split into A and B follows the sheet's separate Dropshipping and POD rows for target audience, pain points and needs. Shared bullets apply to both.

### Persona A: Experienced dropshipper

**Who they are**
- Background listed as "Dropshipping experts, digital marketing, entrepreneurs"; more than 6 months of experience. [LEAD-2]
- Cares more about customer experience and product quality. [LEAD-2]
- Has some background in IT, digital marketing or online selling; learned to sell on their own, usually starting alone and later building a team. [SB-R11 Sees]
- Started because friends were selling successfully. [SB-R11 Sees]
- Vietnam-based (user decision, 2026-10-24; consistent with the sheet's "VN Seller" row and its request for Vietnamese support). [SB-R5 VN Seller] [SB-R8 General]

**Goals and gains**
- Earn a lot of money with a high profit margin and financial independence. [SB-R13 Thinks and feels]
- Find winning products; run ads well; good conversion; good product quality; ship to customers fast with few disputes. [SB-R16 Gain]

**What the lead table offers this group** (these are internal product offers, not goals the persona states)
- ShopBase Dropshipping, marked "(priority)": offer for sellers who have their own suppliers and want to sell DMCA or restricted products (see content risk, Observations; not for Home); lower transaction fee compared with competitors such as Shopify (`UNPROVEN`); register ShopBase payments. [LEAD-2] (Old offer, kept as evidence only. The new USP is in section 7; these are not the USP and the fee claim is not cleared for Home.)
- `SUPERSEDED` (PlusBase no longer exists as a brand): PlusBase Dropshipping, all-in-one, the seller focuses on marketing and PlusBase handles the rest. [LEAD-2] Must not drive Home.

**Pains specific to dropshipping**
- Fulfillment: Epacket is very slow (11 days to reach a buyer in California plus 2-10 days processing). [SB-R6 Dropshipping]
- Poor packaging: Chinese writing on the package, broken products. [SB-R6 Dropshipping]
- Other businesses copy the product being sold and run ads over their own campaign. [SB-R6 Dropshipping]
- Payment gateway "dies" and money is wasted renting another one. [LEAD-2]
- High transaction fee from the current payment gateway lowers profit margin. [LEAD-2]
- Heavy workload: finding suppliers, fulfillment, customer support, operations. [LEAD-2]

**Pains shared with persona B**
- Little resource and time to customize and set up a polished, high-conversion store. [SB-R4 General]
- Little budget for marketing apps/add-ons (upselling, popups) and for polished store templates. [SB-R4 General]
- Too many tasks and too little time to do them one by one (bulk-editing products, copying a store structure to another store). [SB-R4 General]
- Cannot track store performance step by step of the buyer journey (view product > add to cart > purchase) because the current platform is limited or charges for it. [SB-R4 General]
- No Vietnamese-speaking support for store and store-performance problems. [SB-R4 General]
- Difficulties with Facebook ads account, payment, disputes. [SB-R15 Pain / fear]
- Fear of being spied on (sellers spying on each other's products), too many products, an item the sheet words as "Vít đè" (meaning not confirmed, see Open questions; `ASSUMPTION` it relates to rivals "running ads over one's own campaign" in [SB-R6 Dropshipping] [SB-R7 POD]), a language barrier, not knowing how to support customers effectively. [SB-R15 Pain / fear]
- Vietnam-specific (row "VN Seller"; see Q3 before using on an English Home): PayPal and Stripe often rate the business as risky and can hold payments for 180 days, making cash flow hard with a risk of losing the money; Stripe does not support opening accounts in Vietnam, so sellers register with a foreign business identity; spending a lot on ads to drive traffic (the sheet says usually +$3000 and about the first 2 months without profit, so newbies give up easily); Facebook and Google Shopping Ads block ads without enough verification, leaving sellers dependent on ad platforms' updates; staying up at night to watch ads because buyers are mostly in the US/EU. [SB-R5 VN Seller]

**What they need from a platform**
- No setup: a ready store with one click. [SB-R8 General]
- Good Vietnamese support. [SB-R8 General]
- Good conversion on product, home and checkout pages, thanks to the ShopBase team optimizing them and keeping them optimized (worded in the sheet as a need; not a verified product fact). [SB-R8 General]
- Easy to open stores in bulk to test products or designs without editing each page element. [SB-R8 General]
- Support when blocked by Shopify. [SB-R9 Dropshipping]
- A good expert community to support and learn from each other. [SB-R9 Dropshipping]

**What they say and do**
- Prefer to keep their business quiet: avoid showing off the sales dashboard, and are very wary of exposing the store domain because it reveals the products they sell (easy to spy). [SB-R14 Says and does]
- Say that a dropshipper has to be very flexible and adapt to market change every day (Facebook changes UI and algorithm; blocked shops, ads accounts, payment). [SB-R14 Says and does]
- Join dropshipping and POD groups to get help from peers. [SB-R11 Sees]

**Who influences them**
- Friends and peers; word of mouth; Facebook groups. [SB-R12 Hears]

### Persona B: Experienced POD seller

**Who they are**
- POD expert, more than 6 months of experience; cares more about customer experience and product quality. [LEAD-1]
- Note: in the lead table this group is offered PrintBase POD, not ShopBase [LEAD-1]. PrintBase no longer exists as a brand (user decision, 2026-10-24), so that offer is `SUPERSEDED`. Persona B rests on the ShopBase sheet's own POD rows [SB-R3 POD] [SB-R7 POD] [SB-R10 POD]; ShopBase POD is offered in the lead table only to the crossover groups [LEAD-5] [LEAD-6]. **B stays in scope:** the user's audience decision (2026-10-24) names "experienced dropshipping and POD sellers", and A and B are equal (closed, see section 8).
- Shared background and behaviour as persona A (self-taught, joins groups, started after seeing friends sell). [SB-R11 Sees]

**Goals and gains**
- Earn a lot of money with high margin and financial independence. [SB-R13 Thinks and feels]
- Find winning designs and winning products; run ads well; good conversion; good product quality; ship fast with few disputes. [SB-R16 Gain]

**Pains specific to POD**
- Clean (non-trademark, self-designed) products: hard to get orders because the seller must research a specific buyer group's tastes in depth. [SB-R7 POD]
- Trademark (TM) products (designs using artwork or characters or famous brands that carry copyright): easy to get orders with large margin, but the risk of being blocked by ad platforms or sued is very high. [SB-R7 POD]
- Fulfillment: cannot find a supplier or factory fast enough to fulfill thousands of products. [SB-R7 POD]
- Other businesses copy the design and run ads over their own campaign. [SB-R7 POD]
- Not much variety of products at competitive base cost. [LEAD-1]
- Low profit margin. [LEAD-1]
- Clean selling is harder than selling TM. [SB-R15 Pain / fear]

**Pains shared with persona A**: see the shared list under persona A. [SB-R4 General] [SB-R5 VN Seller] [SB-R15 Pain / fear]

**What they need from a platform**
- Permission to sell TM (as written in the sheet; see content risk). [SB-R10 POD]
- Good base cost (product cost price). [SB-R10 POD]
- Good price-support programs. [SB-R10 POD]
- A good expert community to support and learn from each other. [SB-R10 POD]
- The general needs: one-click ready store, Vietnamese support, optimized conversion, easy bulk store opening. [SB-R8 General]
- `SUPERSEDED` (PrintBase no longer exists as a brand): the lead table offered **PrintBase POD**: private product request within 1 month, base cost described as "most competitive" shipped to US, UK and Europe (`UNPROVEN`), private cashback case by case. [LEAD-1] Evidence only; must not drive Home.

**What they say and do / who influences them**: same shared empathy map as persona A. [SB-R14 Says and does] [SB-R12 Hears]
