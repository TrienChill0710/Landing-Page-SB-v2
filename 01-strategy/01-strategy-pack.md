### Primary A · Experienced dropshipper
- **Who / situation:** dropshipping experts, digital marketing, entrepreneurs; over 6 months; cares about customer experience and product quality [LEAD-2]. Some IT / digital marketing / online selling background; self-taught, often starts alone then builds a team; started after seeing friends sell; joins dropshipping and POD groups [SB-R11 Sees]. Vietnam-based (S-USER 2) [SB-R5 VN Seller].
- **Job to be done:** earn well with a high margin and financial independence [SB-R13 Thinks and feels]; find winning products, run ads well, get good conversion and product quality, ship fast with few disputes [SB-R16 Gain].
- **What they believe / experience today (pains):**
  - Payment gateway "dies" and money is wasted renting another; high transaction fee lowers margin; heavy workload (suppliers, fulfillment, support, operations) [LEAD-2].
  - Slow Epacket (11 days to California + 2-10 days processing), poor packaging (Chinese writing, broken goods), rivals copy the product and run ads over their campaign [SB-R6 Dropshipping]. Figures are internal claims.
  - Little time and resource to customize a high-conversion store; little budget for marketing apps and polished templates; too many tasks (bulk edits, copying store structure); cannot track the buyer journey step by step (view product > add to cart > purchase) because the platform is limited or charges for it [SB-R4 General].
  - Facebook ads account, payment and dispute trouble; fear of being spied on; too many products; language barrier; not knowing how to support customers effectively [SB-R15 Pain / fear].
  - Vietnam-specific (kept per S-USER 2): PayPal/Stripe rate the business risky and can hold payments 180 days; Stripe does not support opening accounts in Vietnam; heavy ad spend (usually +$3000, about 2 months without profit); Facebook / Google Shopping Ads blocks without verification; staying up at night to watch ads because buyers are in the US/EU [SB-R5 VN Seller]. Figures are internal claims.
  - No Vietnamese-speaking support for store and performance problems [SB-R4 General].
- **What they need from a platform:** a ready store with one click; good Vietnamese support; good conversion on product, home and checkout pages; easy bulk store opening to test products or designs [SB-R8 General]; help when blocked by Shopify; an expert community [SB-R9 Dropshipping]. These are need statements, not verified product facts.
- **Behaviour / influences:** keeps the business quiet, avoids showing the dashboard, very wary of exposing the store domain [SB-R14 Says and does]; influenced by friends, word of mouth, Facebook groups [SB-R12 Hears].
